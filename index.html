System Architecture & Android Setup

1. Why is this 100% backendless?

Instead of requiring a Node.js/Python server to sign Google FCM OAuth2 credentials, your browser securely establishes a direct WebSocket connection with a distributed MQTT broker. The browser acts as the direct publisher.

2. How does Android stay ultra-low power?

MQTT maintains a single persistent, idle TCP socket with 60-second keep-alive ping packets (only 2 bytes). The phone does NOT wake up or poll continuously; it passively sleeps until the socket receives an incoming packet, consuming less than 1% battery over 24 hours.

3. Pairing with Android

Ensure the MQTT Channel Topic configured above matches the topic string inside your native Android app's MqttForegroundService.kt.
