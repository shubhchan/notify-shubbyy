Player’s Guide

How To Play

  1. 1

    Start the receiver

    On Android, grant notifications, allow battery exemption, then flip Background Listener on. The status should read Connected & Listening.

  2. 2

    Pick a weapon

    Beep fires a chiptune chirp. Siren wails at full volume. Vibrate uses haptics only.

  3. 3

    Press FIRE

    Tap the big button or press SPACE. The message is published instantly over MQTT.

  4. 4

    Watch for the ACK

    The receiver replies on the /ack channel. A round-trip time proves the alert landed.
